  <h1>Privacy</h1>
  <p class="meta">Last updated: September 30, 2026</p>
  <p>This statement describes information handling when browsing, contacting, or administering the EAIS LAB website at eaislab.com. Other websites linked from this site have their own privacy statements.</p>

  <h2>Browsing and public information</h2>
  <p>This site presents our research, members, publications, and admissions information. Browsing does not require registration. Public pages currently have no configured visitor analytics or advertising tracking scripts, and there is no online application or document submission form. Publication searches and filters run in your browser without submitting search terms to our server.</p>
  <p>Member pages display names, research interests, photographs, academic links, and selected contact details. Alumni pages also display thesis titles and graduate destinations. This information is public and may be indexed or cached by search engines. To request a correction or removal of personal information, contact the email address below.</p>

  <h2>Hosting, images, and maps</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Cloudflare Images delivers some images. When pages or images load, these providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request times, and requested resources for content delivery, operations, and security.</li>
    <li>The contact page embeds Google Maps. Loading a map connects your browser to Google, which may receive IP addresses, browser details, and referrer information and use cookies or similar technologies according to its settings. The maps show preset locations; this site does not actively request device location permission.</li>
    <li>Providers determine their processing and retention practices under their policies and service settings. Processing may take place outside your country or region. See the <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement">GitHub Privacy Statement</a>, <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/">Cloudflare Privacy Policy</a>, and <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google Privacy Policy</a>.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Email inquiries and application materials</h2>
  <p>Email links open your email application. If you send a message, the recipient and email providers process the name, email address, message, and attachments you supply. The laboratory uses these materials for the corresponding inquiry, admissions, or collaboration discussion. Send only relevant materials; do not submit private application documents through the public website repository or public links. Contact the recipient with questions about retaining or deleting emailed materials.</p>

  <h2>Administrator login and browser storage</h2>
  <p>The administrator area uses Decap CMS with GitHub authorization and Cloudflare Workers for authentication and image management. The CMS software loads from UNPKG. After login, identity information and access tokens are stored in browser local storage for authorization checks, content editing, and image uploads. This workflow is for authorized administrators; ordinary visitors do not need to log in.</p>
  <p>Published content is stored in the GitHub repository, and uploaded images are stored in the configured image service. Public content may remain in version history or third-party caches; updating a page does not delete every historical copy. You can restrict third-party cookies or clear site data in your browser, but doing so may affect maps or administrator login.</p>

  <h2>Contact and updates</h2>
  <p>For questions about information handling, or requests to access, correct, or remove public information about you, email <a href="mailto:info@eaislab.com">info@eaislab.com</a> and identify the relevant page and request. We will update this statement when website features or services change.</p>
