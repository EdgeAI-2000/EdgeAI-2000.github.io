  On the morning of October 23, 2026, EAIS Lab member [Chun He](/en/people/he-chun/), a master's student in the School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence at Southwest Jiaotong University, gave an oral presentation of about 15 minutes at **DSEI 2026**, the 1st International Workshop on Distributed and Secure Embodied Intelligence for Smart City Applications. The workshop was held with **IEEE MASS 2026**, the 23rd IEEE International Conference on Mobile Ad-Hoc and Smart Systems, from October 21 to 23, 2026, at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Hong Kong SAR, China.

  The talk, **Adaptive Redundant Partitioning for Reliable Collaborative DNN Inference at the Edge**, is by Chun He and [Penglin Dai](/en/people/dai-penglin/) (Southwest Jiaotong University), [Xincao Xu](/en/people/xu-xincao/) (University of Electronic Science and Technology of China), and Hualing Ren (Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications). It discusses reliable collaborative deep neural network inference in dynamic edge environments, focusing on adaptive redundant partitioning and dynamic execution selection.

2026 年 10 月 23 日上午，实验室成员、西南交通大学计算机与人工智能学院硕士研究生[何春](/people/he-chun/)在 **DSEI 2026** 作了约 15 分钟的口头报告。DSEI 2026 即第一届面向智慧城市应用的分布式与安全具身智能国际研讨会（The 1st International Workshop on Distributed and Secure Embodied Intelligence for Smart City Applications），与 **IEEE MASS 2026**（第 23 届 IEEE 移动自组织与智能系统国际会议，The 23rd IEEE International Conference on Mobile Ad-Hoc and Smart Systems）同期举行。会议时间为 2026 年 10 月 21–23 日，地点为香港特别行政区香港理工大学。

报告题目为 **Adaptive Redundant Partitioning for Reliable Collaborative DNN Inference at the Edge**。作者为何春、[戴朋林](/people/dai-penglin/)（西南交通大学），[许新操](/people/xu-xincao/)（电子科技大学）与 Hualing Ren（重庆邮电大学）。报告讨论动态边缘环境中的可靠协同深度神经网络推理，重点是自适应冗余划分与动态执行选择。
