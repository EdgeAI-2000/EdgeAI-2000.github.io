  On September 29, 2026, the **EAIS LAB official website** was launched. The website presents the lab's research vision, team, projects, and publications, and shares our latest news.

  EAIS LAB brings Edge AI into the real world through **system–algorithm co-design**. By connecting hardware platforms, system software, efficient models, and resource scheduling, we build collaborative device–edge–cloud AI systems targeting low latency, low power, and high reliability.

  Our research focuses on three areas:

  - **Multi-Agent Collaborative Perception:** Heterogeneous feature alignment, task-aware communication, and robust fusion across vehicles, roadside units, and UAVs support accurate and efficient 3D scene understanding.
  - **Edge LLM Inference and Deployment:** On-device quantized inference, model sharding across edge nodes, and small–large model collaboration improve inference efficiency under constraints on compute, memory, energy, and network resources.
  - **Agentic AI:** Long-horizon planning, multi-agent collaboration, hierarchical memory, tool use, and embodied skills connect goals, planning, action, and learning to improve task completion and reliable execution.

  We aim to develop reusable, verifiable, and deployable solutions for intelligent transportation, aerial and urban sensing, industrial inspection, and embodied AI. We welcome students and collaborators interested in these areas to explore our research and follow our progress.

2026 年 9 月 29 日，**EAIS LAB 官网正式上线**。网站集中展示实验室的研究愿景、团队成员、研究项目与学术成果，并发布实验室最新动态。

EAIS LAB 致力于以**系统与算法协同设计**推动边缘智能走向真实世界，贯通硬件平台、系统软件、高效模型与资源调度，构建云边端协作的 AI 系统，追求低时延、低功耗与高可靠的智能部署。

实验室聚焦以下三大研究方向：

- **多智能体协同感知：**面向车辆、路侧单元与无人机，研究异构特征对齐、任务驱动的高效通信与鲁棒融合，支持准确、高效的三维场景理解。
- **边缘大模型推理与部署：**面向算力、内存、能耗与网络资源受限的环境，通过端侧量化推理、跨节点模型分片和大小模型协作，提升大模型推理与部署效率。
- **代理式人工智能：**研究长期任务规划、多智能体协作、层次化记忆、工具调用与具身技能，构建“目标—规划—行动—学习”闭环，提升任务完成能力与执行可靠性。

实验室面向智能交通、空中与城市感知、工业巡检及具身智能等场景，探索可复用、可验证、可部署的解决方案。欢迎对相关方向感兴趣的同学与合作伙伴通过官网了解我们的研究，关注实验室的最新进展。

访问官网：[eaislab.com](https://eaislab.com)
